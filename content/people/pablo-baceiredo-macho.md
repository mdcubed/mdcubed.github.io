Pablo has a background in biochemistry from the Autonomous University of Madrid and training in bioinformatics and computational biology at the University of Copenhagen, supported by a “la Caixa” Foundation fellowship. His experience combines experimental neuroscience with computational structural biology, Python-based analysis, molecular dynamics, and machine learning.

## Predicting ligand- and variant-specific GPCR signaling

My research connects molecular dynamics simulations with machine learning to investigate how genetic variation and ligand binding affect GPCR signaling. Focusing on dopamine receptors, the project aims to extract dynamic interaction patterns, activation features, and allosteric communication pathways that help predict functional differences across ligands, variants, and receptor isoforms.
