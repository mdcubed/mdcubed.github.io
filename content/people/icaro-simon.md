Icaro is a computational pharmacologist with a PhD in Pharmaceutical Sciences from the University of Copenhagen and an MSc in Chemistry from UFRGS, Brazil. His experience spans an industrial PhD at SARomics Biostructures and postdoctoral research at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam and the University of Copenhagen. He combines receptor pharmacology, molecular simulations, free-energy calculations, and open-source tool development.

## From molecular mechanisms to predictive drug design

My research asks how ligands, genetic variants, and receptor isoforms reshape molecular dynamics and signaling. I combine structure-based design, molecular dynamics, free-energy methods, and interpretable machine learning to study selectivity and signaling bias. My research program connects precision GPCR pharmacology with light-controlled ligands and allosteric modulation of GPCRs and neurotrophin receptors, aiming to translate mechanistic insight into practical design principles.
