Louise is an MSc student with a background in medicinal chemistry from the University of Copenhagen. She is interested in the intersection of pharmaceutical science and neuroscience, with a focus on drug–receptor interactions. Her master’s research uses molecular dynamics and structural approaches to investigate receptor signaling.

## Biased signaling in serotonin receptors

My research explores how ligands influence serotonin receptor signaling, with a focus on bias between G protein and β-arrestin pathways. I use molecular modeling and simulations to better understand the molecular basis of these differences.
