Simon is an MSc student in pharmacy interested in the intersection of pharmaceutical science and data science. Alongside his university studies, he has pursued additional Python training and developed an interest in molecular modeling and computational drug discovery. He is motivated by open science and the development of reusable research workflows.

## Structure-guided discovery of modulators of an orphan receptor

My project aims to identify peptide and small-molecule modulators of an orphan receptor through structure-based modeling and pharmaceutical data science. It explores peptides derived from the receptor’s extracellular loop and uses ensemble virtual screening to identify candidate small molecules. Molecular dynamics simulations will help prioritize candidates that may stabilize inactive-like receptor conformations for experimental follow-up.
