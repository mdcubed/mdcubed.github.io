Julien studied Biochemistry at Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf, where he completed both his BSc and MSc. He carried out his master’s thesis at the Department of Drug Design and Pharmacology in Copenhagen as an Erasmus exchange student, defended it in October 2026, and continues in the group as a guest researcher. His experience in Düsseldorf’s Computational Biochemistry group includes structure-based drug design and molecular dynamics simulations.

## Ligand-dependent signaling in class B incretin GPCRs

My research explores how peptide ligands influence signaling in class B incretin GPCRs. I combine molecular dynamics simulations with data analysis to investigate the molecular basis of potency and bias between G protein and β-arrestin signaling, with the aim of informing peptide ligand design.
