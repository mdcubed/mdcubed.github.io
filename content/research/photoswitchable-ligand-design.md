---
title: Photoswitchable ligand design
order: 2
summary: Toward predictive design of light-controlled ligands. We aim to combine molecular modeling, machine learning, and free-energy methods in a platform for photoswitchable ligand design.
---

Photoswitchable ligands change their molecular configuration in response to light, offering a way to control biological activity in space and time. We are developing a computational platform to support their design and prioritization.

Our approach combines molecular modeling with data-driven predictions to compare photoswitchable candidates and guide experimental studies. The aim is to make light-controlled ligand design more predictive and accessible across biological targets.

> **Building on published results.** Our published PSW-Designer platform establishes a working foundation for photoswitchable ligand design. This open-source computational platform supports the design and virtual screening of photopharmacological ligands, demonstrating that the core design workflow can be implemented and shared. Building on this result, we aim to extend predictive capabilities and broaden applications to new targets.
>
> [Simon et al., Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.3c01050)
