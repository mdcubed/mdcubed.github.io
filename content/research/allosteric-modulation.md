---
title: Allosteric modulation
order: 3
summary: Exploring positive and negative allosteric modulators (PAMs and NAMs) of GPCRs and neurotrophin receptors to guide selective control of cellular signaling.
---

We investigate how positive and negative allosteric modulators (PAMs and NAMs) can enhance or reduce receptor responses. Our interests span G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs) and neurotrophin receptors, with a focus on understanding selectivity and the modulation of neuronal signaling.

By combining molecular modeling and simulations with experimental collaborations, we aim to understand modulator action and guide the discovery of new compounds. This work explores opportunities to regulate neurotrophin signaling and neuronal plasticity, with potential relevance to brain disorders.
