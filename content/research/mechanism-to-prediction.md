---
title: Mechanism to prediction
order: 1
summary: Connecting receptor dynamics to precision GPCR pharmacology. We combine molecular simulations and interpretable machine learning to investigate ligand selectivity, signaling bias, and the effects of genetic variants and receptor isoforms.
---

We aim to turn dynamic molecular mechanisms into predictive descriptors of receptor function. Our approach combines membrane-embedded molecular dynamics simulations, free-energy calculations, and machine learning grounded in biophysical features. These features include time-resolved contacts, activation switches, helix motions, hydration, and allosteric communication.

Our research spans serotonin, dopamine, and histamine receptors, alongside incretin receptors and an orphan receptor. The goal is to connect simulation-derived features with experimental pharmacology and evaluate predictions on previously unseen ligands and variants.

> **Building on published results.** Published work provides a practical foundation for this approach. In a collaborative study, structure–activity analysis and molecular modeling helped identify β-arrestin-biased serotonin 5-HT2A receptor agonists. These experimentally characterized ligands demonstrate how molecular insight can guide the discovery of compounds with distinct signaling profiles, supporting the feasibility of our mechanism-informed design strategy.
>
> [Poulie et al., Journal of Medicinal Chemistry (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jmedchem.2c00702)
