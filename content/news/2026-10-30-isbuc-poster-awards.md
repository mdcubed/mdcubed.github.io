---
title: Two poster awards at the ISBUC annual meeting
date: 2026-10-30
---

Icaro Ariel Simon and Pablo Baceiredo Macho each received a 2026 Junior ISBUC Poster Award at the annual meeting of ISBUC, the Integrative Structural Biology cluster at the University of Copenhagen.

Icaro presented the work on the hydrophobic tunnel of the 5-HT2A receptor, now published in the [Journal of Medicinal Chemistry](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jmedchem.6c01973). Pablo presented his project on dopamine receptor signaling.

Thank you to our collaborators on both projects, and to the ISBUC organisers and judges.
