title: A UCPH version of QligFEP and QresFEP
date: 2026-11-11

We are developing our own customized version of the Q free-energy workflows **QligFEP** and **QresFEP** at the University of Copenhagen, now with physics adjustments and support for the Amber14sb force field in QresFEP, matching QligFEP.

The code is open: [github.com/mdcubed/Q](https://github.com/mdcubed/Q).
