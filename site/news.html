11 November 2026

A UCPH version of QligFEP and QresFEP

We are developing our own customized version of the Q free-energy workflows QligFEP and QresFEP at the University of Copenhagen, now with physics adjustments and support for the Amber14sb force field in QresFEP, matching QligFEP.

The code is open: github.com/mdcubed/Q.

30 October 2026

Two poster awards at the ISBUC annual meeting

Icaro Ariel Simon and Pablo Baceiredo Macho each received a 2026 Junior ISBUC Poster Award at the annual meeting of ISBUC, the Integrative Structural Biology cluster at the University of Copenhagen.

Icaro presented the work on the hydrophobic tunnel of the 5-HT2A receptor, now published in the Journal of Medicinal Chemistry. Pablo presented his project on dopamine receptor signaling.

Thank you to our collaborators on both projects, and to the ISBUC organisers and judges.

29 October 2026

Julien defends his master’s thesis with the highest grade

Julien Reißmann defended his master’s thesis on ligand-dependent signaling in class B incretin GPCRs on 29 October and was awarded the highest grade. Congratulations, Julien!

Julien now continues in the group as a guest researcher.

15 October 2026

Pablo starts his PhD

Pablo Baceiredo Macho, until now a research assistant in the group, started as a PhD student on 15 October. His project connects molecular dynamics simulations with machine learning to predict ligand- and variant-specific GPCR signaling.
