Mechanistic Drug Design and Discovery

We combine structure-based drug design, molecular simulations, and free-energy methods to understand molecular mechanisms and guide therapeutic discovery.

Our research GitHub

The group

At the interface of computational chemistry, biophysics, and drug discovery. We seek to uncover molecular mechanisms and translate them into predictive strategies for designing better medicines.

01 · Research

Research themes

All research
01

Mechanism to prediction

Connecting receptor dynamics to precision GPCR pharmacology. We combine molecular simulations and interpretable machine learning to investigate ligand selectivity, signaling bias, and the effects of genetic variants and receptor isoforms.

 02

Photoswitchable ligand design

Toward predictive design of light-controlled ligands. We aim to combine molecular modeling, machine learning, and free-energy methods in a platform for photoswitchable ligand design.

 03

Allosteric modulation

Exploring positive and negative allosteric modulators (PAMs and NAMs) of GPCRs and neurotrophin receptors to guide selective control of cellular signaling.
